
I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Zhejiang University's DCD Lab, jointly advised by Prof. [Yang Yang](http://yangy.org/) and Prof. [Jiarong Xu](https://galina0217.github.io/#). I also collaborate closely with Prof. [Carl Yang](https://www.cs.emory.edu/~jyang71/) at Emory University. I graduated from Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University with a bachelor's degree in Computer Science. I have also been a visiting student at Fudan University and the National University of Singapore.

My research mainly focuses on large language models, especially LLM agents, reasoning, alignment, and embodied intelligence. I am particularly interested in multi-agent systems, agentic reinforcement learning, agent benchmarks, environment-grounded agents, and embodied agents such as humanoid VLA. My current work studies how agents interact in social systems, explore and improve through reinforcement learning, and behave in long-horizon tasks with realistic tool use and execution feedback. More broadly, I have a strong background in graph machine learning, including transfer learning, robustness, and privacy.
